Region Definition

Common baseline

pass_base_common, signal trigger, zero veto leptons, zero tau, Njet≥5, MET>250 GeV, HT>300 GeV.

Medium Δφ sideband

Δφ(j1,MET)>0.5, Δφ(j2,MET)>0.15, Δφ(j3,MET)>0.15, and at least one of j2/j3/j4 below 0.5.

Exclusive categories

Medium b-tag multiplicity exactly 1, exactly 2, or at least 3. The sideband is orthogonal to the SR requirement that all first four jet Δφ values exceed 0.5.

Corrections And Inputs

b tagging: 2024 efficiency input was required; all eight bc/light correlated/uncorrelated Up/Down variations are present for all eight background processes.

2025 MC: 2024 MC is reused and scaled only by 110.84/109.82 = 1.009288. No separate 2025 MC correction was invented.

Data recovery: three malformed 2025 flat ROOTs were rebuilt from their original NanoAOD inputs; originals were not overwritten. The complete 7,587-file histogram run has zero weight failures.

Plotting: existing CMS-style plotting code and its 12×8.4 inch canvas were used. Signal overlays are excluded from VR plots.
