I completed the full-2024 study of the TROTA TopResolved impact on the High-dM categories using all 5,489 intermediate ROOT files (230,830,776 events).

Conclusion:
- The recommended next validation target is an exploratory highdm66 scheme. Split the current Nt=0, Nw=0, Nb>=1 block (six recoil bins) into Nres=0 and Nres>=1.
- In the High-dM SR, the Nres>=1 fraction is 69.3% for total background, 81.7% for TT, 34.3% for W+jets, and 35.0% for Z->nunu. The resolved-top multiplicity therefore separates the TT-rich topology.
- In a normalized-yield, statistical-only Asimov diagnostic for this six-bin block, the Nres=0/>=1 split gives Z gains of 1.258x for T2tt(1200,500), 1.105x for T2tb(1200,500), and 1.019x for T2bW(1200,500). These are not full expected-limit results.
- I do not recommend the separate Nres=1/>=2 (72-bin) split before statistics and closure are validated.
- I do not recommend adding Nres to categories that already contain a boosted top/W until exact AK4/AK8 subjet cross-cleaning can be reconstructed.

Important limitations:
- The study weights are genWeight*xsec*lumi/sumw only; post-skim AnalysisSF and a TROTA data/MC SF are absent.
- Adoption requires a TROTA SF and uncertainty, a full AnalysisSF histogram rebuild, LLCR/QCDCR/GCR/DY closure, and a nuisance-aware expected-limit comparison.
